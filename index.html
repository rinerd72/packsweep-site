What it does

Built for the trip you are actually taking.

Real per-traveler logic, real templates, no subscription, no ads. Structure where you want it, edits where you need them.

A list that knows who's packing

Wallets gate to teen and up. Diapers go to whoever is in diapers. Menstrual supplies go where they belong. Every item has rules; you don't have to think about them.

20 starter templates

Weekend, week-long, domestic flight, international flight, road trip, beach, lake, camping, cabin, theme park, cruise, ski, day hike, business, wedding, holiday visit, ministry trip, sports tournament, hospital stay, kids overnight.

Edit the library

Categories, bags, suggestions, and templates are all yours to adjust. Make PackSweep fit your household instead of fighting it.

Checked, not hidden

Completed items gray out and stay visible. The list is a shared record of what's done, not a disappearing act.

Departure reminders

Set a departure time and PackSweep schedules local reminders so the final sweep happens before the car is loaded—not after.

iCloud sync, household sharing

Trips sync across iPhone, iPad, and Apple Silicon Mac through your iCloud. Share a trip with another household member running PackSweep and pack from the same board.

Questions

The useful details.

Is PackSweep a subscription?

No. PackSweep is free to start. PackSweep Pro is a one-time 99¢ purchase—unlimited trips, Apple Intelligence trip tools, PDF export, templates, and app icons. Family Sharing included.

Does it require a PackSweep account?

No. The app does not use a PackSweep account system. iCloud sync and sharing use Apple services when you choose to use those features.

Can two people work from the same packing list?

Yes. PackSweep supports CloudKit sharing for households or shared trips when both people have the app and use iCloud.

Can I customize the built-in lists?

Yes. Categories, bags, items, suggestions, and reusable templates are editable so your family can keep improving the system.

How does it really know what each traveler needs?

Every suggestion has eligibility rules: age band, gender, and care flags (in diapers, uses bottles, takes prescription, menstruates, shaves, wears glasses, breastfeeding). Generate a Beach Trip with an infant, a teen, and two adults and each gets a different, sensible list.
